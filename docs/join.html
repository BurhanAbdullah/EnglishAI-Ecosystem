WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

Bring what you know.

You do not need to be an AI researcher. The project needs people who understand learners, language, software, assessment and evidence.

01 · RESEARCH

Ask better questions.

Experiments, benchmarks, learner studies, evaluation and reproducibility.

02 · ENGINEERING

Make the system work.

Tutor logic, MCP services, interfaces, testing and developer tooling.

03 · PEDAGOGY

Make learning better.

Activities, curriculum, rubrics, explanations and learner-centred design.

04 · COMMUNITY

Make it useful.

Documentation, examples, review, resources and open collaboration.
