Capabilities

Specialists for every part of the English-learning workflow.

Each domain is designed to remain independently testable and replaceable behind explicit contracts.

01 · GRAMMAR

Diagnose, explain, practise

Identify grammatical issues, explain rules in learner-friendly language and generate constrained practice aligned with proficiency.

02 · VOCABULARY

Learn words in context

Support definitions, examples, collocations, word families and recall candidates rather than isolated word lists.

03 · READING

Read with evidence

Combine passage support, comprehension questions, evidence extraction and readability signals.

04 · WRITING

Diagnose before rewriting

Surface grammar, lexical and organisation issues with explanations and revision guidance that preserves learner agency.

05 · ASSESSMENT

Measure learning signals

Generate activities, validate answers, attach skill tags and emit mastery signals without claiming unsupported psychometric validity.

06 · CITATION

Keep provenance visible

Resolve sources, construct provenance records and validate evidence pathways for grounded responses.
