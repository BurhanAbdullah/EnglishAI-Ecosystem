Where can you contribute?

The community is intentionally multidisciplinary. You can contribute code, language expertise, research, design, documentation or ideas.

01

Engineering

Frontend, backend, APIs, databases, local inference, deployment and testing.

02

English & Pedagogy

Grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, assessment, CEFR and learning design.

03

AI · RAG · MCP

Retrieval, knowledge engineering, model integration, MCP servers, schemas and evaluation.

04

Research & Evaluation

Benchmarks, datasets, educational studies, reliability and reproducibility.

05

Documentation & UX

Technical guides, learner experience, accessibility and project communication.

06

Ideas & Partnerships

New learning workflows, datasets, integrations, outreach and research directions.
