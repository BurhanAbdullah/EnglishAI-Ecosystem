We are building an English learning ecosystem.

EnglishAI-Ecosystem brings together learning resources, knowledge retrieval, configurable local LLM inference, MCP tools, assessment and personalised feedback into one extensible platform for English as a second or additional language.

What we are building

A complete learning ecosystem.

The goal is not simply to put a chatbot in front of a learner. We are building the infrastructure around learning: evidence, specialist tools, practice, assessment, learner context and responsible deployment.

01 · LEARN

English learning experience

Grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing and assessment are connected through a single learner-centred experience.

02 · KNOW

Knowledge + RAG

English resources are structured, indexed and retrieved so learning responses can be grounded in relevant material.

03 · REASON

Configurable local LLM

Local means controllable deployment. The operator can choose the model, runtime, hardware, access policy and integrations.

04 · EXTEND

MCP learning tools

Specialist capabilities connect through explicit interfaces for grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, assessment, citation and content.

05 · ADAPT

Learner profile + mastery

Practice and assessment signals can inform progress, feedback and the next learning activity.

06 · TRUST

Provenance + governance

Permissions, validation, source lineage, versioning and auditability are designed into the ecosystem.
