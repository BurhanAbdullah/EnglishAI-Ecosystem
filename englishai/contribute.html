Direct contribution workflow

From idea to merged contribution.

01 · FORK

Choose an area

Find an issue, improvement or new capability that fits the project.

02 · BUILD

Implement

Create a focused branch and make the change with appropriate tests or documentation.

03 · CHECK

Validate

Run the relevant checks and make sure the contribution matches project conventions.

04 · PR

Open a pull request

Explain what changed, why it matters and how it was tested.

05 · REVIEW

Collaborate

Respond to review feedback and refine the contribution when needed.

06 · MERGE

Ship it

Once accepted, the contribution becomes part of the evolving ecosystem.

Contribution streams

Bring your expertise.

Engineering

Web, services, APIs, databases, local LLM integration, testing and deployment.

English & Pedagogy

Grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, assessment, proficiency modelling and learning design.

AI · RAG · MCP

Models, retrieval, embeddings, reranking, MCP tools, prompts, schemas and evaluation.

Research & Evaluation

Datasets, benchmarks, learning outcomes, experiments, reliability and reproducibility.

Documentation & UX

Technical documentation, accessibility, learner experience and information architecture.

Ideas & Partnerships

New learning workflows, integrations, datasets, use cases and responsible collaboration.

Direct contribution and group membership are separate. You can submit an ordinary open-source contribution without signing up for the AI for Language collaboration group.
