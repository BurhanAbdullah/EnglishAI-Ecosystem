Core principle

Local does not mean institution-only.

A local LLM is a deployment choice. The operator controls the environment, model, runtime, access policy and connections. That operator may be an individual, research team, school, university, organisation or another deployment owner.

01

Control

Choose model, hardware, runtime, update strategy and access boundaries.

02

Flexibility

Use the same architecture across personal, research, educational or organisational environments.

03

Integration

Connect approved resources and tools through RAG and explicit MCP interfaces.
