ENGLISHAI FEATURES

Tools designed around language learning.

EnglishAI brings together focused learning capabilities rather than treating English learning as a single generic chat experience.

01

Grammar

Practice grammar concepts, identify errors and work through explanations and corrective activities.

02

Vocabulary

Build vocabulary through contextual learning, practice activities and reinforcement.

03

Reading

Support reading comprehension, language understanding and structured reading activities.

04

Writing

Support drafting, revision and feedback-oriented writing practice.

05

Assessment

Provide assessment-oriented activities and feedback that can inform subsequent practice.

06

English Content

Organise language-learning resources so they can support learning workflows and retrieval.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

AI infrastructure around the learning task.

RAG

Grounded knowledge

Retrieve relevant English-learning material before generating a learning response.

LLM

Configurable local inference

Operate a model in a deployment environment chosen and controlled by the operator.

MCP

Specialist capabilities

Connect language-focused tools through explicit interfaces and capability boundaries.

Design principle: EnglishAI is being developed around language-learning tasks first. AI infrastructure exists to support those tasks, not to replace the learning objective.
